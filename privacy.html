2. Who we share data with

  • Customer ↔ assigned mechanic: during an active job the mechanic sees your name, phone, pickup address and vehicle; you see the mechanic's first name, phone and live location. Before accepting, mechanics see only the area, vehicle model, issue, the short AI summary (if you used AI help) and your optional voice note.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database & login, servers in Mumbai, India), the SMS provider used to send OTPs, Razorpay (payments and automatic refunds), Anthropic (AI answers, only when you use the AI features), OpenStreetMap tile servers (map images; your IP address is visible to them).
  • Legal: when required by Indian law or a valid government request.

3. Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active. Voice notes are deleted automatically 30 days after the booking ends; chat messages, AI questions and answers, and security logs are deleted automatically after 180 days. When you delete your account, your name, phone, saved vehicles, saved places, voice notes, chat messages, AI questions, partner profile and exact pickup locations are deleted or anonymised immediately. Booking and payment records are kept without your identity for up to 8 years for tax and accounting obligations.

4. Your rights & deleting your account

You can view and edit your name in the app, and permanently delete your account any time: Menu → Account & Privacy → Delete my account (both apps). You can also request deletion without the app — see Delete your account. You may contact us to access, correct or erase your data, or withdraw consent.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is restricted per user by database security rules; only our own server functions can be called through the API; sensitive actions are rate limited; payment verification happens only on our servers; the start OTP is never shown to mechanics; mechanics' positions shown to customers before a booking are blurred.

6. Children

The Apps are not meant for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
