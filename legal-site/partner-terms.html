Partner (Mechanic) Terms

Last updated: 18 November 2026

These terms apply to mechanics using the 8Speedy Partner app.

1. Relationship

You are an independent service provider, not an employee. You decide when to go Online. We do not guarantee any number of jobs.

2. Verification

You must provide true identity and shop details. We may verify documents (e.g. Aadhaar, shop photo) by call or in person before activating your account, and may suspend accounts that give false details.

3. Doing jobs

  • Accept only jobs you can reach and repair; release a job immediately if you cannot go.
  • Never start work without the customer's Start OTP.
  • Charge fair prices, agree on the price before starting, and charge at least the minimum visit charge shown in the app only when you reach the customer.
  • Behave respectfully. Harassment, fraud, overcharging or repeatedly releasing jobs may lead to suspension.

4. Location

While you are Online or on a job, the app shares your live location (also in the background, with a visible notification) so you get nearby requests and the customer can track you. Go Offline to stop sharing.

5. Payments

Customers pay repair charges directly to you. Any platform commission will be announced in advance in the app.

6. Deleting your account

Menu → Account & Privacy → Delete my account, or see Delete your account.

7. Law & contact

Governed by the laws of India; courts at Durgapur, West Bengal.
