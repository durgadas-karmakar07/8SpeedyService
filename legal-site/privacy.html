We do not use advertising IDs, do not sell personal data and do not use your data for ads.

2. Who we share data with

  • Customer ↔ assigned mechanic: during an active job the mechanic sees your name, phone, pickup address and vehicle; you see the mechanic's first name, phone and live location. Before accepting, mechanics see only the area, vehicle model and issue.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database & login, servers in Mumbai, India), the SMS provider used to send OTPs, Razorpay (payments), OpenStreetMap tile servers (map images; your IP address is visible to them).
  • Legal: when required by Indian law or a valid government request.

3. Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, your name, phone, saved vehicles, partner profile and location are deleted or anonymised immediately. Booking and payment records are kept without your identity for up to 8 years for tax and accounting obligations.

4. Your rights & deleting your account

You can view and edit your name in the app, and permanently delete your account any time: Menu → Account & Privacy → Delete my account (both apps). You can also request deletion without the app — see Delete your account. You may contact us to access, correct or erase your data, or withdraw consent.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is restricted per user by database security rules; payment verification happens only on our servers; the start OTP is never shown to mechanics.

6. Children

The Apps are not meant for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
