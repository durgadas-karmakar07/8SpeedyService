Delete your 8SpeedyService account

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This applies to both apps: 8SpeedyService (customers) and 8Speedy Partner (mechanics), by 8SpeedyService.

Delete inside the app (instant)

  1. Open the app and log in.
  2. Customer app: open the menu (☰) → Account & Privacy → Delete my account.
    Partner app: top-right menu (⋮) → Account & Privacy → Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. You are logged out and your data is deleted immediately.

Request deletion without the app

Send a WhatsApp message from your registered mobile number to +91 96352 97320 saying "Delete my account". We verify and delete within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Name, phone number, saved vehicles, saved places, free credits
  • Partner profile: name, phone, shop details, photo, location
  • Booking notes, voice notes, chat messages, AI questions and answers, and reviews you wrote
  • Exact pickup addresses and map points (old bookings keep only the area, rounded to about 1 km)

What is kept

Booking and payment records without your name or phone number, for up to 8 years, because Indian tax and accounting law requires it. If a mechanic is on the way or you are on an active job, finish or cancel it before deleting.
